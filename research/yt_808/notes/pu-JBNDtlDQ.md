# pu-JBNDtlDQ — Fearless Beatz — "How to make HARD 808s in Serum" (Serum 2, 9 min)
Source: transcript.
- Osc: sine, **−3 oct** from C3; alt "digital FFT" table, WT pos 2–4 adds harmonics. Mono. (S)
- Amp: attack up slightly, **S 0, D ~2 s**; release up. Serum compressor. (S)
- Transient, three methods: (1) splice the transient of a Spinz 808 onto the body, aligned at the waveform peak; (2) LFO env → **coarse pitch, range 24 st**, short; (3) a second wavetable **+12 st** with short level envelope (click/knock layer), osc randomization off "so it always hits the same". (S)
- Processing (sampler): 1 voice; **detuning a few cents** off-zero can sound better (S); sampler S 0, decay 8–10 (s); **Ableton Roar** (digital mode); linear-phase EQ cut of a resonant bump; maximizer to "maximum"; normalize to 0 dB. (S)
