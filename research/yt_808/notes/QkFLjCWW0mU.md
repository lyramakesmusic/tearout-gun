# QkFLjCWW0mU — Dom (VST Instruments & Plug-Ins / Production Lab) — "Create HUGE 808s With FM Synthesis" (HALion FM Lab, 17 min) — **FM architecture reference**
Source: transcript.
- Body carrier **op8 sine, coarse ratio 0.5** (so the played note sounds an octave down; ~41 Hz at the low note). Mono, **legato, retrigger off, glide on**, pitch-bend ±12 st. (S)
- Pitch ("tone") envelope on the carrier: short upward start → perceived transient; depth by ear. (S)
- **Harmonics without distortion**: op7 (**ratio 1.0, i.e. 2× the carrier**) → op8 and op5 → op8 (second modulator); index raised by ear on the analyser, **kept low enough that the fundamental stays dominant**. (S)
- **Delay the modulators' attack** (slower attack on op7) so harmonics bloom *after* the hit — keeps the transient clean. (S)
- Impact layer: **op6 sine at fixed 100 Hz, very short decay** (key-independent "shock" = the kick hit). (S)
- Attack layer: op4 (short env) modulated by op3 → mid-high "sharper" transient. (S)
- **Sub reinforcement: op2 plain sine** carrier added because FM harmonics thinned the fundamental. (S)
- Click: **op1 with max self-feedback = noise**, very short envelope. (S)
- Movement: one user envelope on **all modulator levels (index envelope)** → evolving tone. Then light compression + light distortion. (S)
- Claim: FM lets 808s cut through a heavy mix with no saturation. (S)
