# sYpXT-UxIsA — Bass Computer — "In-Depth Guide to Making 808 Basses From Scratch" (Ableton Operator, 36 min)
Source: transcript only.
- Osc: single **sine**, all other operators off. Mono (1 voice) — "multi-voice 808 is a mud generator". (S)
- Amp env: decay ~**3.4 s** for long version; length adjusted to taste. (S)
- Pitch env: start **+12 st** (octave) at 60% env amount (≈ +7 st effective), decay **~500 ms** to root; says +4 st also works. Rationale: drum skin tightens on impact. (S)
- Glide: legato/portamento **115 ms**; glide only when notes overlap, else full re-attack. (S)
- Harmonics: Saturator drive / Ableton Pedal with wet/dry. Spectrum: fundamentals in the 30s are inaudible without overtones; good 808s show overtones 100 Hz–1 kHz. (S)
- Transient: second operator **triangle at fixed frequency**, short envelope = "tap" at front; alternatively use it as FM modulator on the sine for a "real clear tap". (S)
- Kick layering: acoustic kick sample, LP'd, amp-shortened, fundamental ~40 Hz; checked that kick and 808 fundamentals don't overlap. **Delay 808 attack ~20 ms** so kick owns transient. Distortion on 808 only (not post-sum) preferred. (S)
