- Good 808 samples are already lightly saturated (slight buzz). (S)
- **Frequency split at ~500 Hz** (high-shelf/low-shelf at 500): low <500 untouched, high >500 distorted. Whole-808 distortion = "really fuzzy"; high-only = "gentle fuzz". (S)
- Crossover warning: overlapping shelf filters → **comb filtering / muffled sound**; nudge filters until the sum is flat. (S)
- Idea: 3-way split (low clean, mid normal distortion, high fuzz). ReaPlugs: Distortion (gain, hardness), Fuzz (hard limit = "fatness"), Waveshaping distortion (gentle buzz → crank), JS Saturation. (S)
- Trap metal = most distorted 808s (whole-band). Pop Smoke "Dior" cited as drill reference. (S)
