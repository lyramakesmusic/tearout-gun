# FWSEVAtHiGA — Zens — "How To Make Your Favorite 808s" (9 min) — rage/plugg-ish sample-processing
Source: transcript.
- Osc: Serum sine with a few added additive harmonics (harmonic editor); "darker" ones get one extra partial. (S)
- Pitch: LFO envelope mode → coarse pitch, **anchor** (one-direction) shape = "adding a kick to the 808". (S)
- Gain: boost into **Serum/FL clip** ("boost it right up and hit the clip button"). (S)
- FX: Fast Dist; **transient shaper ("Punch") to tame/raise attack**, tail lowered; kick transient from library pitched down, LP'd heavily, shortened, layered; Thermal/Cold Fire (multi-stage distortion); **brickwall limiter slam** as the shortcut to the glued kick+808 sound. Modulation FX (chorus/flanger) **only on mids/highs via multiband**. Decapitator (cuts lows depending on setting). Infiltrator / Devastator 2 multiband distortion. MidSide plugin to **mono the bass below a chosen freq**. (S)
- Sampler: Edison/Fruity Slicer stretch for length — time-stretch also adds texture. (S)
