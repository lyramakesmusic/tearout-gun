## Gun bass (3 layers, 140 BPM)
1. **Sub layer:** sub osc + white noise, **"completely destroyed" with Tube distortion**; small sharp LFO on noise and sub level (fast decay of noise); LFO1 → master tune at **−7** (pitch drop).
2. **Kick layer:** kick sample → Serum FX: distortion → EQ → compression → **Comb filter** (cutoff, res, drive) → becomes tonal bass shot.
3. **Percussion layer:** perc shot → distortion → **Serum Comb filter**.
4. Stack all three → machine gun one-shot (Marauda style).
- Supporting: high screamer layer automated (wide, high end).
