# D9Hbz0Hze_0 — PRO TIP: USE HYBRID REVERB ON YOUR DUBSTEP BASSES (Wheysted Music, 10:42)

**Relevance:** High for convolver technique (short IRs from one-shots as tonal layer).

## Technique
1. Ableton **Hybrid Reverb**, Blend set to **convolution only** (reverb section off).
2. Load **a one-shot as the IR** (drum, gunshot, "whale" sound, other bass shot) — convolution = "adding the reverb of another sound".
3. **Decay pulled way down / small size** so the IR is very short → imparts tone, not space ("just getting the tone of maybe another bass shot mixed into it"). Long decay on rhythmic basses smears into later hits.
4. Run in **parallel chains, wet 100%** each (dry already present); low end EQ'd out of the wet chains; use built-in IR EQ low/high cut to restrict band (e.g. only low-mids).
5. Watch stereo: convolution gets wide fast; narrow with the width control; pre-delay default 10 ms (adjust to offset).
6. Try different algorithms (Serial/Parallel).
7. Sum 3 layers + Camel Crusher on sum + sub → compress → **resample/freeze together** to capture interacting resonances.

DSP: parallel FIR convolution with short (tens of ms) arbitrary-sample IRs, HP-filtered, mixed under dry.
