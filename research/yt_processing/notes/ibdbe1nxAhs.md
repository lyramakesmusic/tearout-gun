## Screech bass (reverb screech)
1. Envelope (fast "screech" shape) → FM amount from osc2 (osc2 level down, pitch up); osc2 hard-sync also on the same envelope.
2. White noise, level on envelope ("sounds like someone screaming … will really crunch up when we add distortion").
3. Filter 1 high-pass, cutoff on envelope; noise routed through it.
4. Filter 2 (filter1 → filter2 serial): notch blend, blend at middle, envelope swing.
5. FX: Distortion cranked → Compressor (attack & release up) → Chorus (feedback down slightly; "shreddy and stereo") → Reverb ("so it sticks around").

## Machine gun bass
1. Snappy pluck envelope on level, faster, retrigger only on notes (not looping).
2. Wavetable "Clicky Robot" (Glorkglunk factory set).
3. Distortion (WT position also modulated) → multiband compressor (mix maybe a bit lower).
4. Optional white noise.
5. **Pitch modulation via envelope** for the gun "zap" (amount to taste).

## Under-bass
LFO on level (rhythmic), wavetable → multiband comp → bit of distortion → delay (slapback).

## Top laser wobble
Sine + FM from second sine + sync; triplet-8th LFO; distortion in **downsample** mode; low cut; multiband comp ("way more palatable"); pitch bend; vary downsample amount and sync for variations.
