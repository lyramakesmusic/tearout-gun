# w7H7P2VMnVs — HOW TO TEAROUT DUBSTEP (GUNSHOT BASS) SVDDEN DEATH, EXCISION, MARAUDA, ODDPROPHET
Channel: XLNTSOUND | 20:06

**Relevance:** Medium. Gunshot bass built inside Serum; emphasis on comb + chorus + short delay/reverb for metallic character. Mostly pack promo/vamping; few hard numbers. Tempo 145 BPM.

## Chain (Serum, in order)
1. Osc A: metallic/digital spectral wavetable ("Shraps/Strats" table, "a lot of harmonics"); sharp envelope → level and WT position.
2. FM from osc B (basic shape, level down), B up an octave → more metallic.
3. Filter: **Comb** — resonance moving slightly (modulated), small cutoff movement → main metallic source ("the main points is usually the comb filter").
4. Distortion — a little drive.
5. Multiband compressor (Serum OTT-style) — "to make it louder"; add envelope release so tail rings.
6. EQ — peak boost for "bite", slight high pass.
7. Chorus — key "metallic" device: mix explored at 100% then reduced; **feedback down**, depth up, **short delay**, rate low; placed after compressor. Wants it "super metallic at the very beginning then chill out".
8. Delay — very short slapback, ping-pong, captions say "16 … 30/36" [inferred: ~30–36 ms time, low feedback ~36%?].
9. Second filter: **Comb− (negative comb)** and/or **Reverb filter** ("reverb filter … short reverb that makes it sound scary") placed after EQ.
10. Reverb — Hall, **small decay, small size, low dry/wet**, low cut up (no lows).
11. Hyper/Dimension — a little; detune optional.
- Unison trick: detune ~16, **random phase down** → voices start in phase → more percussive/"phaser-like"; LFO on detune.
- Sub: triangle osc, **direct out, mono**, no automation sometimes better.
- Drop processing: "fat rack" (XLNT's own processing rack) + EQ "poor man's disperser" (stacked EQ bands, preferred over Disperser for control); joke "17 fat racks".

DSP: comb filter (feedforward/feedback delay at 1/f0) with modulated feedback = metallic resonant peaks; chorus with short delay & feedback ≈ modulated comb; short ping-pong delay ≈ another comb/doubling; small hall/reverb filter = short diffuse IR.
