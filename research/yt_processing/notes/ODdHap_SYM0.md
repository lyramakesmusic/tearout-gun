# ODdHap_SYM0 — How to Create a Tearout/Dubstep Gun Sound!
Ghosthack · 6:06

**Relevance:** On-topic: tearout gun rack from percussion samples with EQ3-disperser + convolution-ish reverb.

## Chain
Sub: Serum "analog BD sine" + filter crunch for overtones; no FX. Two pitch modulations: fast envelope pitch drop at start (kick-like punch) + slow LFO wobble; drive in filter.
Gun rack (Ableton), on percussion samples (need a sample with some low end), sample transposed (+26 st sweet spot? "on 26"):
1. Pedal (Ableton Pedal) distortion.
2. Compression.
3. Disperser = several EQ Three in series; low/high split frequencies mapped to Macro 1; **MIDI envelope modulates the split frequencies** (moving dispersion = key), plus rate/LFO on freq.
4. Hybrid Reverb, "car garage" IR preset (convolution), only size + decay changed; short decay fine.
5. EQ: boost mids around **1.6 kHz** ("the interest of the sound inside the reverb").
Variation by transposing main sample + changing disperser frequency.
Clap: -5 st, overdrive, mid EQ boost, reverb.
