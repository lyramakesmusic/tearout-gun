## Chain (FL Patcher)
**Patcher 1 – mid/side EQ (FL preset):** low cut (on the sides) [inferred]; duck two low-mid peaks on the sides; small dips in the mid for annoying frequencies.

**Patcher 2 – Two-band frequency splitter (FL preset), crossover dragged down to split only the sub** [crossover value not stated; inferred ~100 Hz]:
- **Low band:** OTT → OTT → OTT → CamelCrusher → out. Alternative: sub → CamelCrusher only (amount turned down, its compressor optional) for a cleaner sub.
- **High band:** **Disperser, amount at max, pitch/frequency set by ear** → OTT ×3 → CamelCrusher → out
- **OTT settings (each): Time 1000 (max), Depth 50%**
- **CamelCrusher: default preset, Amount 50**
- Optional: a third band with a tiny reverb on the top band only.

DSP: 2-band crossover; the high band gets an allpass cascade (Disperser) → 3 cascaded 3-band up/down compressors at 50% depth with the slowest time constants → tube/mech-style distortion (CamelCrusher = 2 distortion types + LPF + comp). Three OTTs in series at 50% are roughly a single heavy upward+downward compressor with more noise-floor lift.
