**Relevance:** Generic dubstep bass processing with stock Ableton; short, few numbers. Marginal-on-topic.

## Chain
1. Serum bass patch.
2. OTT (Ableton Multiband Dynamics "OTT" preset).
3. Saturator (with Erosion before/around it to fill top end).
4. Second OTT → lots of noise; cut that noise (EQ/filter) [numbers not given].
5. Another Saturator, cranked hard.
6. Limiter right after.
7. Group; short reverb to taste ("super short" decay).
