Channel: Black Marvin (psytrance producer) | 22:12

## Presets (Multipass, 3 bands default)
1. **Multiband distortion**: Distortion (Overdrive algorithm — least harsh; lower algorithms progressively harsher) on **mid band** (= "density/body") and **top band** (= "clarity/crispiness"); **low band untouched** (low cut earlier anyway).
2. **Multiband transient shaping**: transient shaper on mid + top bands of a bass; **not on low band** — introduces artifacts; not gain-compensated.
3. **Multiband gating**: Trance Gate snap-ins with different patterns per band (low/mid/top) for rhythmic contrast.
4. **Multiband micro delay**: different delay rhythms per band (e.g. mid rolling, top 2/16), LFO smooth-random → pan, inverted between bands, rate 8/16.
5. **Multiband convolver games**: Convolver with Eternia "delay tricks" IRs (Fractal Sounds) on mid band, mode Stretch, fade out; mix LFO-modulated 0→up (sine, 3/16 or 2/16 sync) for gated rhythm; then multiband delay after.

DSP: crossover → per-band nonlinearity/dynamics/time FX → sum. Leave low band clean (linear).

**Techniques tags:** multipass, overdrive_stack, convolver, multiband_comp, mono_low/sub_separate
