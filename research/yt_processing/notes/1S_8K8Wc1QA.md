# 1S_8K8Wc1QA — How to Make A SUB DISTORTED For TEAROUT & Dubstep (Clean & Loud) (Dripment, 8:23)

**Relevance:** Medium-high. Sub processing for tearout (separate sub layer).

## Chain A — distorted sub
1. Serum sine; LFO (trigger, 1/4 rate, smooth to stop clicks) on sub + a noise layer ("J60 analog" noise).
2. Serum Distortion: **soft clip**, "good drive", **80% wet**.
3. Ableton **Pedal**: output −2.5 dB ("negative 250"), **Overdrive** mode, Bass +35%, Mid −100%, **Sub switch ON** (removes mids/highs).
4. **Saturator:** drive +13 dB, soft clip on.
5. **EQ notches** ×2 to remove mid content.
6. **Limiter** ceiling 0 dB (at end, cleanup).

## Chain B — clean sub (used in "Eradicate")
1. Serum sine, Distortion soft clip **62 drive, 100% wet**.
2. **Erosion** wide-noise, high amount → adds highs.
3. **Overdrive:** 30% dry/wet, 13 drive, filter band moved to mid-high.
4. **Compressor "Brute"** preset (Ableton).
5. **OTT** (Kompany's settings preset).
6. **Notch EQ** cleanup.
7. **Limiter** to 0 dB.
- Sub tricks: Auto Pan phase 360°, synced rates (1/4, 1/2, 1 bar) = amplitude wobble; route MIDI from machine-gun bass to sub so sub follows the gun.

DSP: sine → soft-clip (adds odd harmonics) → further clipping, then notch the mids so harmonics sit only near the sub and in highs.
