## Chain (machine-gun bass)
Layers in a group: (1) sub layer, (2) bass layer, (3) transient layer, (4) tonal layer.
1. Sub: Serum sine on sub osc + noise for grit + Tube distortion "to glue"; LFO controls noise volume and pitch (pitch drop).
2. Bass layer: any bass sample ("washy" types preferred).
3. Transient layer: percussion sample; EQ boost at its peak point; Disperser (Kilohearts) or "10+ EQ Threes" in series.
4. Group bus: "variety of saturators, OTTs, glue compressors" (fat rack).
5. Resample to audio → manipulate pitch, warp, pitch bends, stretch.
6. More processing on resample: disperser again ("less digital, more watery ... adds dope transience"); band-pass "phone filter" fills.
Yoi bass: triangle wave → notch filter sweep → downsample (key element).
Evil screech: sustain + arp → resample → clip pitch bend.
Pan snare: triangle wave layered on snare.
