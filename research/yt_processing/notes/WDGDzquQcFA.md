# WDGDzquQcFA — TEAROUT GUN BASSES POST PROCESSING MADE EASY
Channel: Wheysted Music | 9:43

**Relevance:** High. Post-processing of a Serum tearout gun: double disperser → convolver (clap IR) → OTT → parallel small-room reverb.

## Chain (Ableton, in order)
0. Serum gun patch + a little **GClip** (soft clipper) at the end.
1. Widener (Wider/Utility) — patch was too mono in the mids; widen.
2. EQ — add some high end.
3. **Double Disperser**: two instances at different frequencies — low one (emphasises sub/transient), high one (clicky "ugh" bass character). Placed **early** in chain "so other things can affect it". Alternative: stacked EQ Eight bands macro'd (EQ-Eight disperser).
4. **Convolver / Hybrid Reverb with a clap or snare sample as IR** (e.g. a "Tracy" clap); use **the front/transient of the IR** for best tone; length/position of IR changes result drastically. Recommend running it **parallel with the dry**; put the widener on the convolver path so the convolved tone is wider than the dry.
5. OTT (light) — "glue compression for both of those"; lets highs come out, "without it sounds more bricked".
6. Parallel **small-room reverb rack**: Small Room preset, **very low decay**, cutoff higher, **low cut** (no lows), rack dry/wet to 100% on the reverb chain → "bed".
- Bonus: sampler sustain mode loop on a tiny region with correct root note turns a gun into a guttural sustain; then low cut, OTT, saturation, high boost.

DSP: two allpass cascades centered at different freqs; convolution with a percussive transient (clap) IR = short broadband comb/resonant coloration; parallel stereo-widened wet path.

**Techniques tags:** disperser/allpass, convolver, reverb_on_bass, OTT, softclip, resampling, sub_sidechain_or_split, eq_after_dist
