# Op1nyQxm540 — HOW TO GET CLEAN DISTORTED DUBSTEP BASSES
Wheysted Music · 8:16

**Relevance:** High. Core principle for heavy-bass distortion: distort the sub and mids TOGETHER into one clipper, EQ the "pain points" before distortion.

## Chain, in order
1. Sources summed into one bus: sub (sine, optionally with Kickstart sidechain ducking) + mid bass (square→saw rhythm synth) — or Nasco-style shots (harsh highs). Demo: a sine + a sine **4 octaves above** distorted together = "the classic dubstep synth".
2. **EQ before distortion:** cut the low-mid "pain point" just above the sub (mud buildup) and resonant/whistly highs ("hissing"); turn down the sub band a bit because it's being pushed to clipping.
3. **Distortion: digital clip** (Ableton Saturator "Digital Clip" curve [inferred]) — "a little more aggressive of a clip". One shared stage.
4. Amount of distortion controlled two ways: drive AND sub level into the clipper — more sub → the low energy dominates the clip, sound gets more low-heavy and grittier on top (intermodulation).
5. No further post processing in the demo track besides a frequency shifter on the sub in one part.

## DSP translation
Summing sub + mid then hard-clipping = intermodulation distortion: the high-amplitude sub modulates the clip threshold seen by the mids, producing sub-rate "chug" grit and fusing layers. EQ-before-clip sets which bands generate harmonics.
