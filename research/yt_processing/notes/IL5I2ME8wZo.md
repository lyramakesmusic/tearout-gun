# IL5I2ME8wZo — HOW TO MACHINE GUN IN VITAL LIKE MARAUDA, SVDDEN DEATH TEAROUT DUBSTEP
Channel: Letsynthesize · 11:05

**Relevance:** High. Tearout machine-gun anatomy: a heavily crunched sub+top group plus an FM "character" layer, with an explicit ~500 Hz mid-boost before the crunch.

## Architecture
Gun = (A) **distorted group: sub + top noise/tone crunched together** + (B) **FM "character" layer** (metallic percussive top, lighter distortion).

### (A) Sub + top → distorted group
- **Sub (Operator):** sine, amp envelope + **pitch bend envelope** = "a kick basically, a punch on the sub"
- **Top (Vital):** factory wavetable "Drink the Juice"; envelopes + LFO on WT position (sync/warp mode); **comb filter** (filter 1) and **LPF** (filter 2, "clears up the sound"); Vital distortion; filter that **boosts heavily in the mids and cuts the lows**
- Top post chain: **"EQ Three rack" disperser** (many Ableton EQ Threes; the crossover phase shifts stack up into a Disperser-like effect: "adds a nice little block to transients") → EQ → **OTT** → EQ → **Saturator** → EQ ("after the saturator you still need to clean up") → **Flanger** → Reverb rack + volume pull-back
- **Group (the crunch):** EQ with **very heavy mid boost ~500 Hz** ("cuts through the mix") → **Saturator, Analog Clip mode, lots of gain** → "this is where the crunch happens"

### (B) Character layer (FM)
- Vital: random osc + sine **+38 semitones** FM'd by osc2; osc3 = **saw in sync mode, very high frequency, (24/48)**, also FM → clicky transient
- **FM depth high at the transient, decaying over the note** (more FM at onset, filtered/lighter tail); every FM amount has its own envelope
- Filter on the tail → reverb → EQ for more mids
- Post: distortion (brings up sustain vs transient) → **frequency shifter** (tuned together with pitch, by ear) → EQ (freq shifter creates unwanted frequencies) → EQ heavy boost **~500 Hz** → EQ Eight boost highs → **Glue Compressor with very long attack and heavy compression** (re-creates the transient lost to distortion) → Saturator with **makeup at max**

### Final group
EQ (tame excess mids) → **transient shaper** → Saturator → sidechain.

DSP: "Analog Clip" saturator = soft/hard-knee static clipper; a 500 Hz peak before the clipper makes the clipper generate dense intermods around the mids; slow-attack compressor = transient enhancer (lets the attack pass before gain reduction kicks in).
