## Growl chain (Vital + post, in order)
1. Init saw.
2. **Disperser** (Kilohearts, or stock: macro many EQ Three/EQ bands — free rack) — "moist" bubbly phase smear. Sustain patch uses **4 dispersers**.
3. Filter: low pass with **resonance cranked** (for tonality) — or high pass with some resonance + LFO for vocal/growly movement; second filter (filter 1 routed through it) for slight attack opening that interacts with the disperser.
4. Distortion: **Hard Clip** mode (Vital); "Digital" mode found super clean.
5. Chorus — "on freeze" (rate frozen), voices/levels down, mix lower → slight stereo + tonality.
6. Multiband compressor (Vital OTT) — release up.
7. Duplicate disperser after to restore "moisture" lost through compression.
8. Phaser — frozen, mono → mid-range "vocal" fatness (on one layer).
9. Post: **GClip "the heck out of everything"** (free hard/soft clipper); optional softer setting. Subtronics designs sounds "that are clipping or work well with clipping" → loud master.
10. OTT "for more sauce", **Wider** (free) for super-wide.
- Separate clean **sub layer** under it; "gives a bit more fatness when you clip it at the end".
- Pitch-bend down on sustains for "dragged out gnarly" effect.

## Metallic basses
- Sine + FM (osc up 12 / an octave), then feedback-resonant FX: Chorus frozen with feedback, feedback delay **tuned** (Vital delay mode 2 for precise pitch), **comb filters** ("resonance and how you tune it"), then distortion; reverb mix automated up for throws. "Get some FM tones and then feedback them to heck."
- Low-pitched sine FM → warbly siren (FM at sub-audio carrier = fast pitch bending).
- Distortion+multiband on heavy stuff gets "gross in the low end" → **scoop it**.

DSP: allpass cascade → resonant LPF/HPF → hard clip (digital) → frozen chorus (static short modulated delays = fixed combs) → 3-band up/down comp → allpass again → final hard clip on bus.
