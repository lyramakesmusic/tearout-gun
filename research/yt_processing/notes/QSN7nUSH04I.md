**Relevance:** Low/partial. Mostly a mixing/mastering walkthrough (excluded per scope). Kept only the per-sound bass-processing facts below.

## Bass sound-design bits
- Fake-out phrase: resampled, swept filter + flanger, chopped "bits of gold", heavy transient shaping.
- Chugs (quarter-note dubstep): **OTT + EQ**, Fresh Air (high exciter / noise), a **Disperser**, then mix-lane chain "compression → EQ shaping → GClip". Chug group: low cut + soft clip.
- **Frequency shifter / pitch shifter varied per hit** so repeated chugs aren't identical; also slide audio start (sample offset) per hit.
- Width: chugs wider than sustains; sustains narrower (mid). Basses occupy ~**200 Hz–18 kHz**; sub **fully mono** (sample sub, utility only).
- Soft clip (GClip) on every group ("at least 70 GClips").
- Excluded (mixing): hot mix to 0 dBFS, LUFS −2 to −1.5 target, multiband parallel 25%, inflator 50%.
