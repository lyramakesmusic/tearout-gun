# LRQFCUXqXr4 — SERUM 2: HOW TO TURN ANY SAMPLE INTO A HEAVY DUBSTEP BASS
oddprophet · 11:02

**Relevance:** Directly on-topic: sample→tearout gun in Serum 2 with stacked overdrive + split-band convolver.

## Chain
1. Sub: sine osc ("all feeling no sound").
2. Osc B = spectral wavetable loaded with tonal percussion (ride cymbal etc.), dropped 1 octave. Percussive samples work best.
3. FX: **stacked Overdrive modules first** — "you can stack the overdrives on top of each other and it creates this major crunch".
4. Splitter (bootleg Multipass) → Convolver on HIGHS only. Medium IR, e.g. "rock plates"; bring decay down, IR gain up.
5. More distortion after the splitter.
6. Multiband compressor (OTT-style).
7. Pitch envelope: LFO2 → global main tuning.
8. Scan knob = sample playback speed/direction.
9. Filter: band-pass/notch ("bandpass notch my favorite"), route the spectral osc through filter.
10. Add more overdrive to taste ("compressor needs a little bit more").
11. Warp modes on spectral osc for variation.
Cleanup note: lots of stereo in lows → would need mono-ing lows; low end gets over-driven, "take out the sub and replace it".
