**Relevance:** On-topic tearout drop breakdown; layer structure + group processing (OTT/Erosion/compression).

## Chain
Bass = 4 layers grouped: (a) percussion layer (punch), (b) secondary percussion (texture), (c) saw bass (mid/high), (d) 808 sub; plus wide noise.
Group bus: OTT (NOT at 100%, "wacky" time values to retain punch) → Erosion (grit, on wide noise) → "Punch" compressor preset → sidechain.
Layer (a) percussion: door-shutting/explosion sample pitched -12 st; Resonator tuned to D# (key) → Little MicroShift (width) → Frequency Shifter (some resonance, shift up since sample was pitched low) → Gate (snappier) → EQ Eight: remove all low end, lots of high end → Erosion → sidechain. Chopped middle section; at -7 st then shifted up an octave.
Layer (b): EQ remove sub/bass first → Utility to mono (right channel only) → MicroShift (wide) → parallel chain: top = heavy reverb + sidechain, bottom = dry; Gate after to stop reverb tail.
Layer (c) saw: Serum env1 mapped to sync + level → gritty stab; both osc -3 oct; reverb filter; OTT.
Sub layer (808): chopped; pitch hiccup bend right before snare; descending pitch on last hit. Another sub copy: Erosion, highs stripped → noise hiss → OTT → EQ → Erosion → Punch.
Extra resample of main bass → phaser + EQ8 low cut, blended in background.
Snare: "pot pan" snare, no lows, lots of reverb, saturated.
