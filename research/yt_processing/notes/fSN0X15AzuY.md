# fSN0X15AzuY — These Mistakes Make Your Basses WEAKER! (Dubstep Post-Processing & Sound Design Mixing Tutorial)
Channel: Bunting · 18:53

**Relevance:** Moderate-high. Dubstep bass post-processing in Vital/Ableton with explicit OTT technique, disperser DIY, phaser/notch placement. Leans "clean/fat" rather than tearout-crushed, few numbers.

## Chain / rules (in order he builds it)
1. Notes in octave 0 ("the bassy range").
2. Source: wavetable with strong midrange (roundish square shape); limit FM amount (high FM pushes energy to highs, loses beef); lower the FM octave.
3. If a high-pass/filter sweep removes low-mids → route a **sine sub on its own output, unprocessed** (sub bypasses FX).
4. Phaser/flanger: fewer movement FX; phaser **mono**, centered in **low-mid range**; turn mix down if keeping it.
5. Distortion (Vital): push until it audibly crunches, then **dial back slightly**. Lowering the distortion's bass/low knob lets you push further with less crunch.
6. Vital multiband comp: attack & release up (cleanest).
7. In post — Saturator: push to just peaking over 0 dB on its meter then dial back.
8. **OTT:** raise **Time** (less artifacty), drop **Amount to ~50%**; **two OTTs at ~half amount sound cleaner than one at 100%**.
9. EQ: gentle, wide moves — mild mid scoop + slight high boost; avoid steep big scoops; use scale knob to tame. Narrow notch for a specific problem frequency.
10. Stereo widener (Polyverse Wider) ~**50%**; more erodes mono fatness.
11. Mid/side EQ: on sides a mid scoop + top boost.
12. End of chain: limiter, glue comp, or **soft clipper** (push volume without overs). Limiters for clean sounds; gritty sounds tolerate soft clip.

"Secret sauce" add-ons:
- **Phaser in the low-mid range placed before the OTTs** (try pole counts) → adds low-mid harmonic fullness.
- **Disperser** (DIY: Ableton EQ Three ×many in a group, all cutoffs on one macro) → "bubbly and lasery", smooths over-grit; placed at end of chain on the saw example.
- Erosion (noise mode), width up, amount down, before the main effects → noise fill.
- Ableton Overdrive with low settings at end → top-end crispness.
- **Notch filter (dramatic mid scoop) BEFORE distortion** → much fatter.
- "OTT light": Ableton Multiband Dynamics default compression preset instead of OTT for cleaner wubs/leads/pads.

Example: plain saw → OTTs → phaser (low mid) → disperser at end = "obese".

DSP: OTT = 3-band (Ableton default crossovers ~88 Hz / ~2.5 kHz [inferred, Ableton OTT preset]) upward+downward compression; Time scales attack/release; Amount = global wet/depth. Two half-depth OTTs = less aggressive per-stage gain riding than one full. EQ Three disperser = cascade of 1st/2nd-order crossover filters whose summed phase response acts as allpass.
