# twT4wEypWt8 — "How To TEAROUT - Machine Gun Dubstep Tutorial [Like Marauda, Svdden Death, Sullivan King]" — Avant Samples (26:18)

FL; preset-based (Lux "Heavy Dubstep" pack). Value: Lux's mixer chain + **swing/off-grid** timing + **sub wall**.

## Pattern (main point)
- **Swing / hand-placed notes**: "you can't get the tearout gun sound without a lot of swing in your notes"; perfectly quantized "sounds abrupt, doesn't flow". Lux "places them by hand, doesn't care about the grid".
- 145 BPM; kick/snare halftime (snare every other kick). Notes pulled down in pitch on some hits for variation. 16-bar drop with 8-bar halves; last bar cut for a fill; reverb throws.
- Layer 2 = second gun preset with same MIDI **+1 octave**; later variation: clone an octave down layered with original.

## Lux mixer chain (gun bus)
- **Sausage Fattener** → EQ (cut all lows, sub added separately) → Valhalla Room (off; reverb throws) → **Kilohearts Formant Filter** ("huge part of his sound design", subtle tonalities) → **Gross Beat sidechain** + **Kickstart** (fast) → reverb (off) → **Disperser** (Lux: "really important") → Kickstart.

## Sub
- **"Sub wall"** (Svdden Death / Marauda technique): long sustained sub note (D) following kick/snare (sidechain ducking via Gross Beat + Kickstart halftime) **instead of following the gun rhythm**.
- EQ low/mid boost → **Ohmicide** → **iZotope Trash** (distortion stacked) → Kickstart.

## Other layers
- Background long note: Sausage Fattener (fatness/color high), EQ low cut + **2–3 kHz boost**, OTT, Formant Filter, Gross Beat, Disperser, **"robotic delay" — time all the way down (very fast)**, cutoff down, level ~half = "robotic chrome" metallic, short reverb, limiter.
- Main gun LFO **decay lengthened** slightly to make the cut less aggressive.

## Master
