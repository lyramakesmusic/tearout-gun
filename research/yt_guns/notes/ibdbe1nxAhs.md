- Wavetable from **"Glorkglunk"** bank: **"Clicky Robot"** — "makes it clicky and fat".
- Distortion, WT position tweaks, **multiband compressor**. Optional white-noise LFO (prefers cleaner).
- **Pitch modulation** via LFO 2 ("glazier puke") / also envelope → pitch. 

## Screech patch (relevant FM spice)
