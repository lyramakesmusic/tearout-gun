# 2Ox-rSTms9o — "How To Make TEAROUT GUNS & REMIXES!! (MARAUDA x EPTIC - Wall of Death)" — Dripment (25:28)

Ableton remix; sample-layer guns + clipped sub. Same "Dripment method" as LM5LgEvjNHs, with more numbers.

## Sources
- Gun samples (Splice/artist packs), bass one-shots, **metallic bell** tonal samples, claps/drums for transient; all **transposed to root (D#)**; EQ8 cleanup on every layer.
- Some layers: loud rack + **reverb with short decay (≤1 s; 600 ms used)**, low wet; some dry — mix wet & dry layers.

## Sub
- Plain sine (Serum basic shapes) → **Saturator lots of drive** → **EQ8 low-pass** (clean, loud; translates to phones). Target ~**-9 LUFS** on the sub; lows bus -3 dB.
- Resample sub root note; chop into triplet hits with **short audio fades** — length & fades shape the rhythm ("as much as you can fit, still a little fade").

## Gun group
- Low cut at **160 Hz** (sub owns <100 Hz) → reverb tight decay → Multiband Dynamics **25%** → Saturator **5 dB, 33% wet** → GClip at 0 dB, 2× OS.

## "Mixology room" (sub + guns)
- **GClip +6.3 dB, softness 23%** on sub+guns together ("glued, crunch") → Multiband Dynamics **20%**.

## Main group / loud rack
- **3 Dispersers, moved right (higher freq) to let lows through**; can be tuned to root. Effect: "lasery on the transient, beefier at the beginning — which is really what a gun is — plus mid-range grunge".
- Loud rack 3: OTT → Saturator → OTT → Saturator (100%) → EQ8 boosts **167 Hz** and **6.8 kHz** → Glue comp (**makeup 5 dB, threshold -9 dB**) → Utility → JST clip → Multiband Dynamics **15%** → JST clip **+2.3** to 0 dB.
- Fake-out FX: automated filters, reverb dry/wet.

## Pattern
- **Two-bar call-and-response** phrases in a **16-bar drop**; second 2 bars speed up; **triplet grid (1/8T)** mixed with straight 1/8 and 1/16; Marauda "a don in the triplet grid". Second drop = faster rhythms + different fills. 130 BPM here.
