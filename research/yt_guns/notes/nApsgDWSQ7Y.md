## Layer framework for the machine-gun bass
- **Sub / bass / transient / tonal** layers in one group, processed together, then **resampled**.
- Sub: Serum **sine on sub osc** + a little **noise** + **tube distortion** to glue; LFO controls **noise volume and pitch** (per hit).
- Bass layer: any bass sample (washy preferred) — swapping sample changes tonality.
- Transient: **percussion** sample; EQ boost at its peak point; **disperser** (Kilohearts) or "10+ EQ Threes" trick.
- Group: saturators, OTTs, glue compressors ("fat rack"/Bass Hack).

## Pattern / manipulation after resample
- Resample to audio, then **pitch bends**, warping/time changes, **stretching**, rhythm programming.
- **Filtered fill**: band-pass ("phone") version before hitting full drop.
- Disperser on the resample: "less digital, more watery, adds transience".

## Other tearout elements
- Snare: **triangle wave layered on snare** ("pan snare").
- Screech: arp + sustain, resampled, pitch bend.
- Yoy: triangle, filter notch sweep + **downsample**.
