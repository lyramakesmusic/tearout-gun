# -4EJhV7Skfs — "How to make a Deathstep Growl and Machine Gun Bass Step by Step Part 1" — Synoid Dub (24:12)

NI Massive. Part 1 is the **growl** that part 2 (mxCxFuOokjY) derives the gun from. Older deathstep approach.

## Body (growl foundation)
- Single oscillator; wavetables recommended: **Strontium**, Chrome, Scrapyard, Wicked, Bronze. Mode **Bend** (not Spectrum), intensity down/negative (lower pitch emphasis).
- **Voices 4 with "restart via gate"** = unison voices phase-locked (no chorusing, +level → turn down), more saturated.
- Pitch/detune of unison voices with restart on: sweet spots **0.30** and **0.70** → creates internal movement.
- **Ring mod** with restart + phase manipulation for growl movement.
- Serial filters; **Classic Tube**; **Dimension Expander** (small); EQ cut mids at ~10 o'clock; high shelf bump; **Sine shaper** + **Parabolic shaper** to taste; mono, glide.
- **Scream** filter with resonance; **Double Notch** with **mono LFO, 1 bar, sine** on cutoffs; envelope on top of LFO; WT position mod.
- Tape hiss/bright noise for fullness.

## Mix advice
- Keep bass mono; layer 3–4 tweaked copies for stereo; check in mono. Don't clip more than **3 dB** into master.
- F as common bass key.
