# -Z5YunR-upo — "HOW TO GUNGROWL - FL STUDIO TEAROUT SOUND DESIGN" — Silvyr (13:25)

Serum 2 + huge FX chains; "90% of what you hear is effects". Growl hybrid, but a full gun-adjacent signal chain.

## Body (Serum 2)
- **Monster** wavetable, **-3 octaves**, (retrigger/phase not random).
- LFO1 → **volume** (floor at **34%**, not to zero), **WT position (bipolar)**, and a **band-pass filter (bipolar), key-tracked**, res **18%**, cutoff **137 Hz**; also a bit into distortion.
- 2x **overdrive** stacked; 2x multiband compression (**18 dB** gain); comb filter "a bit messier".
- Macro → WT position (only).

## Sub
- Duplicated patch (same LFO) with sine; warp **Bend +** ~**22%**; **white noise high-passed** (avoid mud) to **44%**; overdrive **37% → 87%** (+50 via mod). "Distortion plays a big role."

## Body insert chain
- Multipass (distortion + **comb filter for stereo ~40%**) → OTT → EQ (low cut) → **Disperser** ("lots of disperser; makes it sound like a monster growling, liquid") → OTT (lower depth) → EQ → crunched reverb (white-noise-like crunch) → Disperser (tames resonances) → EQ.
- Sub: only EQ + **mono**.

## Group
- OTT on gun+sub → Disperser — "this is already 80% of the work".
- Parallel: disperser pinched hard → reverb 100% wet → EQ → frequency shifter → Soothe → wide.
- EQ dip low mids/highs → clipper → **Freeform Phase** (disperser-like) → EQ → **King Tubby**? (captions) → Soothe → clipper.
- Bass bus: EQ, King Tubby, disperser×2, EQ, clipper, +1 dB high shelf, linear phase, Soothe → OTT → 18 kHz cut → high exciter → Ozone (mono low end, imager) → EQ (duck low mids, bring out sub) → Soothe → clipper → limiter. "Most of my mixing is clipper then limiter."
- Warning: over-processing makes the bass too big to layer anything else; stop around group stage.
