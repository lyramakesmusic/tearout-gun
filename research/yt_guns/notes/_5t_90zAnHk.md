# _5t_90zAnHk — "How to Marauda | Tearout Dubstep Tutorial" — DaniMusiX (23:55)

Full track; drop gun is Serum + resample + Vital sub. Track-level Marauda conventions.

## Tempo / arrangement
- **138 BPM** (Marauda's stated favorite). Abrupt drop; snare/clap build that accelerates; tape stop; pitched TTS vocal ("Mastodon") processed with Erosion (sine), OTT, two Redux at different rates (50%/30%), fast delay.

## Gun (Casket VIP-inspired)
- Serum: high **square** (basic shapes), **LFO1 → FM from osc 2** (sine/square with unison; stereo unison via matrix); small sparkly WT; white noise.
- **Two comb filters, automated by LFO1** ("really important").
- Distortion with **pre-filter enabled**, automated by LFO1; chorus for messiness.
- Resample → stretch → **frequency shifter** → lots of overdrive → glue comp w/ makeup → stereo.
- Layers: "trash" sound with **short delay set to the right pitch** (tonal), metallic higher hits, sub (Vital sine + bit of white noise, multiband comp, distortion, OTT, Utility mono, EQ, glue/soft clip).
- Resample again to make the rhythm, chop; **very short delay to make it more tonal**; boost ~**100 Hz**; saturation.
- Fills: sine + square through compression/distortion/saturation.
- Other drop part: Vital basic waves **FM from osc 3 (very low sine)**, unison WT, white noise, LFO2 random amp; overdrive, OTT, **frequency shifter "really important — weird startling low sound"**, "EQ Three disperser", hard-curve saturation; a **bell** through overdrive layered.

## Drums
- Kick+snare; claps **volume-automated to feel sidechained** (kick transient then snare tail); metallic snare layers.

## Recurring
- "Short delay to add tone" stated as his classic trick, used 3+ times.
