# JYeBhtNFdB8 — "Dubstep Gun Bass made from actual Guns" — Dodge & Fuski and Virtual Riot (7:44)

Field-recording vlog; little detail on processing, but confirms the sample→tonal method.

## Source
- Recorded Glock 19, rifle with **subsonic vs supersonic** ammo, bolt-action .22 (sub/hypersonic). Multiple mics (SM7B/"kick drum mic", iPhone "sausage" mic for crunch, field recorder for echo); layered into crisp one-shots. Subsonic = quieter pop (subtle transients), supersonic = loud snap.

## Turning bangs into tonal bass
- "EQs to **boost the lows** a lot, compressors, **resonators**, **very short delays** to turn this atonal bang into something that has a note — especially a very short delay with **a lot of feedback**" (= comb filter by delay).
- Longer notes keep the gunshot's echo/tail (natural reverb) audible.
