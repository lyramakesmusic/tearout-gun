# fJZYQPGFXB4 — "How To Make a TEAROUT DUBSTEP DROP (Tearout Gun Sound Design & Screech Tutorial)" — DRIZKO (13:06)

Ableton + Serum + Kilohearts. Numerous concrete values. **3 elements: tonal layer, transient layer, sub**, then resampled together, then "gun rack".

## Sub
- Serum sine (basic shape) + a few harmonics, **tube distortion**.
- **Short envelope → coarse pitch at 19** (semitones) = transient on sub.
- LFO2 → level and noise osc (gated per hit).
- Post: EQ with **huge notch in mids**, big low boost, slight high boost → Saturator **1 dB soft clip** → freeze/flatten.
- Separate final sub too: sine + harmonics, **LFO → coarse pitch** for attack, EQ + saturation.

## Tonal layer (resampled)
- Serum: **"Scout"** wavetable (Spectral), **unison 16, detune almost 0**.
- Modulation at **1/8 notes** → level and **high-notch filter** = "really short punch".
- FX: Hyper/Dimension, **Diode 1 distortion** (mix modulated), multiband comp, **Reverb filter** (Serum FX filter "Reverb" type) with **modulated cutoff** = "real tonality of this sound".
- Resample → OTT (output up, **time up, amount down**) → reverb → **Frequency Shifter, frequency mode -100 Hz** → **Kilohearts Transient Shaper** (more attack + pump, **clip on**) → EQ (cut annoying, boost mids/highs) → reverb → Saturator **4.5 dB soft clip**, output down → **frequency shifter -35 Hz**.

## Transient layer
- **Short percussion** (Cashmere pack) → transient shaper (attack+pump) → OTT → reverb → frequency shifter (tonality) → EQ → saturator.

## Gun rack (on resample of all 3)
- EQ low cut (separate sub) → OTT (output up, **time ~1000%**, amount **30%**) → EQ mid cut → Saturator **2.29 dB** → EQ → Compressor makeup **3.5 dB** → OTT → EQ → **Kilohearts Frequency Shifter automated for variation across the drop** → Saturator soft clip → EQ → Transient Shaper (clip on) → EQ "radio" bandpass at drop start then bypassed → transition reverb.

## Screech (spice)
- Serum init saw **-2 oct**, FM by another saw; LFO → FM + level; osc2 pitch going up; pitch automation ±**3 st**; **comb filter 56 Hz** high res; Hyper detune max mix **21%**, Dimension size 0 mix **35%**; tube, chorus, multiband; OTT; **auto-pan 12 Hz**; saturator **2.8 dB**; reverb.
- Tension layer: D# vs E (minor 2nd), two digital WTs, comb, fast bend-/PWM mod.
- Cut low end on the sides of wide layers.

## Drums
- Kick low-mid dip; big snare; **China cymbal** sample adds power.
