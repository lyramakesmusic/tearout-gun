# ftwBNJP1tgs — "HOW TO MACHINE GUN BASS LIKE MARAUDA (MASTADON), TEAROUT DUBSTEP" — Letsynthesize (6:43)

Serum + Ableton Operator 808. Clear conceptual recipe: **make a snare/percussion in the synth → comb filter for tonal tail → distort → heavily distorted 808 sub underneath**.

## Transient
- **Pitch envelope** on attack is "very important" (Matrix env → **master tune**) = transient/pitch blip.
- EQ after distortion "turns the sound back into a blocky/percussion thing" (the "magic trick").

## Body
- Osc A wavetable **"Monster 4"** (any WT works; changing WT changes whole sound). **Env/LFO → level**: short decay, no sustain.
- Osc A warp **FM (from B)** / "bend +/-" [captions: "a band plus minus warping mode" → **Bend +/-**] — any warp mode works.
- Osc B **saw**, **+3 octaves**, modulating osc A (FM). **Unison 5 voices on the modulator only** (osc B) → carrier stays mono but output wide/noisy.
- Serum **sub osc** on: critical — "when you distort, the sub adds crunch". Shows that disabling sub loses rumble.
- FX: compressor → distortion (noise-ish) → EQ → **comb filter** "adds the tail" = machine gun. [In Serum FX filter: comb]
- Low cut "a little higher" to match 808; highs boosted; saturation; final **Saturator soft clip as limiter** (keeps transients).

## Sub (808 layer)
- Separate track receiving gun MIDI (Ableton MIDI from routing). Operator **sine**, filter **PRD** (ladder emulation) with filter drive **+21 dB** (captions "121"), soft shaper; overdrive; **digital clip**; **LPF ~300 Hz**; pitch envelope (inaudible after distortion).

## Group
- Chorus, OTT, EQ, saturator at end.
