# Rapv1lcXagA — "HOW TO MAKE TEAROUT DUBSTEP LIKE NIMDA" — Swampy (17:46)

FL + Serum + Multipass. Credits the "**oddprophet method**" repeatedly.

## Main gun (Serum)
- Wavetable from a **bass loop/one-shot dragged into Serum** (his Mudkit pack WTs) + sub + **band-pass filter**.
- **LFO1 → WT position, level, cutoff, sub level, "high EQ" gain** (one LFO drives all).
- **LFO2 → master pitch** = "transient effect, the laser — it really needs it".
- Distortion on.
- Processing: Disperser → **Multipass** ("gives it everything — literally the oddprophet method") → EQ → **Fruity Balance automated** to choke tails on fills (stop lingering).

## Transient / layers
- **"Big Gun Snare"** sample in every song: **transient shaper going crazy → frequency shifter → 1–3 dispersers → GClip (soft)** → EQ low cut. Changing its envelope from transient to sweep for other sections.
- oddprophet free "synth dirt" samples layered **with and against** the bass, only freq shift / low cut — subtle fill.

## Sub (clean approach, unlike oddprophet's sub-in-gun)
- Fat sine + noise osc + heavy distortion. **LFO1 → master tune, fast, envelope mode = transient**; LFO2 slower → master tune for movement (later **two triangles up and down**).
- Saturation (dynamics & DC filter off), EQ mud cuts, slight low boost.

## Sustain/spice
- Two sines (one sync, one FM-ing), distortion → freq shifter → Multipass automated in B-half (screechy) → saturator → Multipass with phase distortion → disperser → OTT → EQ → Fruity Balance.
- Reversed Muerte bass shot, Multipass, freq shifter automated every other hit.

## Variation / pattern
- Macro morphs LFO shape instead of new patch. Convolvers "chaotic" → **Stereo/utility width to zero (mono) then re-add width via Dimension Expander**; **automate convolver mix** with the bass.
- Fills: resample whole drop into Edison, tape-stop mangling ("mud pies"), grab snippets.
- Master: GClip + Maximus.
