# 1033mUjb1oI — "How To Make Tearout Guns (SYZY - Grease)" — RYZEN (9:50)

FL Studio; sample-based gun. Simple.

## Transient / body (layer 1)
- **Gunshot sample** (Cymatics), **time-stretched to BPM** (stretched/smeared), pitched **down** a little, sample start and fade-out adjusted.
- Low cut first.
- Serum FX on it: **all-pass filter** ("Allpasses" in Serum FX filter), cutoff down, res up a little = "the main thing"; **chorus**: delay 1 ~small, delay 2 = 0, depth ~max, feedback 0, LPF max, mix **50–51%**, rate **0.9 Hz**; OTT; **multiband** comp on.
- EQ cuts to taste.

## Sub (layer 2)
- Sine (FL basic shapes), short volume envelope + **pitch envelope** with a little attack for "stomp".
- Fast Distortion maxed, EQ: highs up/mids dropped.

## Group
- Sub + gun routed to one bus → Patcher: Serum FX **Soft Clip drive ~68**, compressor (not multiband — multiband sounded worse), stereo widen **14%**, LP above **18 kHz**, low dip slight.

## Spice (layer 3)
- **Glass breaking** SFX shortened, sidechained.

## Pattern
- **Pitch automation** clips on both gun and sub (pitch bends per phrase). Drums at **75 BPM** (half-time 150).
