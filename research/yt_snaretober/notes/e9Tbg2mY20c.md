## Snare = 3 layers
1. **Clap** sample: EQ (pre) **boost 1.11 kHz**, cut very lows, very highs, and ~5 kHz → **Overdrive** → OTT with time turned up (less noisy tail) → Drum Buss (punch, compression) → frequency shifter (reshape) → EQ (post) → reverb → glue comp. Band-pass around **1.1 kHz** = "clappy frequency" (1–2 kHz).
2. **Noise layer** (Operator noise): release matched to clap length; low cut ~**1 kHz**, reverb, overdrive, EQ cuts at 5–6 kHz.
3. **Snare for punch**: high-passed at **~700 Hz**, tail chopped; transient + a little low end only.
## Bus
- Drum Buss with transient knob up; pull trim instead of a pre-utility to avoid over-saturation.
