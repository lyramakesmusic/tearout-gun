## Layers [said]
Three roles: (1) **transient "beef" layer** — very loud, very fast attack ("layer it with a clap"); (2) **tonal layer** — another snare / in tearout a **pan snare**; (3) glue — glue compressor over the stack. Can be 1–5 layers.
## Rules [said]
- Avoid reverb stacking: pick layers with little baked reverb, control space globally.
- Key: find the snare's peak with EQ8 narrow band; e.g. peak reads **F2**, fits D minor. In key = "harmonically perfect", deviation allowed later.
- Sidechain: in riddim the kick plays with the snare, so snare is effectively already sidechained; in dubstep give the snare its own sidechain.
- Snare/clap on beat 3 (half-time).
- Transient shapers if snare lacks bite in mix.
