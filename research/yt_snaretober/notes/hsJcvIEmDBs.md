# hsJcvIEmDBs — Floure District, "How to make a Snare out of ANYTHING | FloureTober Day 3" (Phase Plant + Snap Heap)

## Layers
1. Single noise click [said: "just a noise click"]; everything else is FX.

## Chain [said+screen]
1. Distortion (louder).
2. Nonlinear Filter, mode METALLIC [screen] ("messes up the sound").
3. Convolver (Snap Heap) with "Norlin Spring" IR, shortened a lot, feedback all the way up; cloned into a 2nd convolver in parallel bus and STRETCHED (~55–68% stretch on screen) — stretching shifts the IR's frequency range to tune the ring [said]. "Really good on pan snares."
4. Highpass with a huge resonant peak [said].
5. Stereo shaping: cut part of the fundamental in sides [said].
6. OTT ("pretty basic").
7. Multiband transient shaping: low-band sustain turned down a lot (cleanup), high band attack up for punch [said].
8. Clipper.

## Takeaway
Noise impulse → metallic nonlinear filter → short high-feedback spring convolution (ring/tail generator) → HP peak → OTT → multiband transient shaper (kill low sustain) → clip.
