# x5OPiKNA-BI — Stinger | Hive Audio, "How To Make Custom Snares, Claps, And Rims Using Serum" (transcript only)

## Snare [said]
- Osc: Analog BD Sine; level driven by LFO1 (envelope mode, BPM off) shaped as a curved decay; LFO rate ~2.1 Hz (≈ 475 ms cycle).
- Noise oscillator ("Alpha and Z" noise) on same LFO1 → level.
- Filter: High 6 with drive, noise routed through it.
- Serum FX: Distortion X-Shaper → Compressor (multiband for grit) → Reverb Plate with LFO on mix (reverb only as long as the snare).
- Post: Saturn, Fruity Waveshaper (to tame harshness), EQ (low cut), Soothe2.
## Clap [said]
Noise only; LFO "teeth" with 3–4 drops, last tooth only to mid level = tail; LFO ~6.2 Hz; Tube distortion, compressor.
## Rim [said]
Noise only; amp env skinny, decay 40–60 ms (50 ms chosen); tube saturation; EQ low cut + low-mid boost.
