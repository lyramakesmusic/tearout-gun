## Envelopes
- Body amp: LFO1 envelope mode, **rate 1/4**, smooth off, hand-drawn: 100% then fade into tail (on screen roughly linear ramp down over the cycle) [said+screen 135].
- **Pitch env: LFO2 envelope, rate 1/32, depth 65 st** (70 caused a gap at the start) [said]. Concave curve: pitch falls faster at start [said].
- **Noise: LFO3 envelope, rate 1/8, drawn as a very sharp spike** — drops to ~0 within the first ~5% of the cycle [said+screen 190]. Reference waveform shows ~4–5 chirping body cycles under the noise.
