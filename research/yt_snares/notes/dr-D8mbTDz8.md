# dr-D8mbTDz8 — Letsynthesize, "DON'T BE STUPID WHEN YOU LAYER SNARES"
Archetype: layering rules. Said.
- If two bottom layers: **tune them to the same note** (spectrum analyzer lowest harmonic: e.g. A2 vs C -> retune).
- **Don't put the low/bottom layer at the very start** — the original snare has no LF during the transient; move the bottom later (a few ms) and phase-align it. "You don't want to mess with the transient."
- Check polarity (flip phase and compare).
- Prefer **saturator over glue compressor** to even out; glue attack ~10 ms for chunkier transient. EQ before saturator to reduce lows so they distort less.
- HPF right below fundamental (~216 Hz in example) "can mess up the low end" — he prefers not.
- **Use one bottom, one transient, one tail**; multiple bottoms or transients mess up the sound.
