# BaTmdjAmZwU — ARTFX, "Dubstep snare tutorial! (Skrillex, Zomboy, Virtual Riot, Barely Alive...)"
Archetype: brostep/Skrillex-era sample-layered big snare with long gated-ish reverb tail. Ableton. Sample layering, not synthesis.

## Layers (said)
1. **Transient layer**: Vengeance dubstep snare #234 with fade-out (pitched +1 st). Only layer with no fade-in.
2. **Character layer**: clap (Breaktweaker clap #7), "much lower in volume than the transient".
3. **Noise layer**: white noise from Operator, faded in; supplies the tail.
- **Timing**: clap and noise are *faded in during the transient* so the transient owns the headroom ("otherwise you stack transients on transients"). Fade-in spans the "mush of frequencies" before the low body starts. (said)

## Processing (said)
- Transient layer: EQ boost **~200 Hz** ("punches you in the chest"), HF boost, Glue compressor to control peak.
- Clap: low cut only. Noise: low cut + HF boost.
- Bus: EQ +200 Hz, **mid scoop** for "hollow" sound, HF boost, roll off super-highs; multiband comp (highs most, no lows; nothing below 120 Hz); transient shaper (attack up, then drive pulls level back); CamelCrusher (tube dist + comp), CamelFat.
- **Reverb**: noise layer sent to room reverb ("clear chamber"), a little of bus too; reverb EQ'd to **highs only**, then gain boost. Long reverb tail is "the characteristic of Skrillex snares".
- Master: CamelCrusher, CL1B compressor, Rough Rider (LPs ~16-18k), Ozone: small mid reverb, harmonic exciter, multiband (M/S), EQ boost mids + **~660 Hz** "dirt", maximizer **+8 dB** (IRC III).
## Takeaways
- 3-role model: transient / character (clap) / noise-tail. Non-transient layers fade in after the click.
- Snares pushed to ~8 dB of limiting: loudness by clipping/limiting is part of the sound.
