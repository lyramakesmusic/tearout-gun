# nD1QlBuz3w4 — Deucez, "How To Actually Make Dubstep Snares"
Archetype: dubstep snare tail extension. Said.
- Base snare has great attack but no tail -> take a clap through **lots of reverb, resample only the reverb tail** (cut the dry clap), EQ (more highs, upper-mids), lay under the snare **faded in after the attack**, fade out. Short tail variant too.
## Takeaway: tail = reverb-only resample faded in after the original transient (pre-delay style).
