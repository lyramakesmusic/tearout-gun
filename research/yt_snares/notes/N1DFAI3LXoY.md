# N1DFAI3LXoY — Code: Pandorum, "3 ways to build the PERFECT SNARE for Dubstep, Tearout, Deathstep, Metal"
Archetype: tearout/deathstep sample-layered snare; "pan snare". FL Studio. Said only (no numbers beyond EQ).

## Model (said): **"Talk, tail, and tone — the three T's"** (talk = transient/click "attack talk", tail = noise, tone = pitched ring / pan).
- **Talk**: snare sample with good initial attack, cut short, EQ to bring out fundamental, **transient processor for "really aggressive click"**, mono.
- **Tail**: a mostly-white-noise snare tail; remove its talk; reverb (wider/brighter); **low cut**; **boost 1.6-1.8 kHz** — "crucial for any snare... the character, the tone... the frequency that punches through the mix". Boost highs for piercing tail; light distortion.
- **Tone**: snare with metallic tone, low cut, **boost the metallic resonance, distort** ("resonance and distortion go together"), reverb, low cut again after distortion. Pitching the tail down creates crazy sounds.
- **Pan snare from any snare**: EQ — find one spectral peak in the snare and **boost it aggressively with a narrow peak** -> pan/"trash can" tone; then distort, reverb (pre or post distortion), low cut so it doesn't disturb the talk.
- Snare tool in Phase Plant: snare1 / snare2 / pan layer with pan pitch control.
## Takeaways
- Tearout "pan snare" = a strongly resonant narrow peak (single tuned partial) + distortion over a normal snare. Pan pitch is a primary knob.
- Mono transient; stereo/reverbed tail.
