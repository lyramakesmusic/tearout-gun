# q5RKF1DPV_U — XLNTSOUND, "Top 3 Ways To Create Skrillex Style Snares 2023 (Quest For Fire)"
Archetype: Skrillex QFF-era tight "dead" snares + metallic ring. Serum + Ableton. Said values.

## Waveform reading (said)
- Snare = click transient (broadband info) -> **"the note"**: a clean sine-like section (the body tone) -> white-noise "release" which is much longer.
- Trick: take a dubstep snare and **pitch up +12 st (not warped)**, add fade.
## Serum "snare starter" preset (said)
- **Triangle or sine ~200 Hz** for punch. Envelopes in **LFO Hz mode**, macro "length" scales rates.
- **Pitch env: very fast (LFO rate ~5.6 Hz in envelope mode) on coarse pitch, amount ~11 st**; "laser attack — don't want it too lasery".
- Noise: **"JP106 HP noise"** (high-passed Juno noise), **attack slightly later**, super-long release.
- FX: low-pass ("low 6"?) filter with fast LFO on cutoff -> pluckier; **EQ boosting the body fundamental** ("we want more attack, throw one on the gain"), a notch, high shelf gain enveloped up quickly; OTT; distortion.
- Going up an octave requires shortening length (macro).
## Layering
- Add a tiny glass "ping"/perc (Skrillex "Tears" and 100 gecs snares have this **extra resonance**, short, slightly later than the transient) -> "body point".
- Layers should be **phase-aligned**; out of phase "sounds weird and won't cut through". Fade if off.
- **Metallic tail = short room reverb/delay** (Ableton Reverb with reflections up, diffusion, chorus; Hybrid Reverb with small size), resampled, EQ'd to keep only metal, crossfaded onto the tail end. "Dead" tight snares that still have reflectiveness.
- Transient for snares should be **darker** than hi-hat transients.
