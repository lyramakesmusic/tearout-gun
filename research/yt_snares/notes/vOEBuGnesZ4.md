# vOEBuGnesZ4 — Rocket Powered Sound (Blaqout session), "Layering Tearout Drums"
Archetype: tearout snare stack with Corpus "frying pan" chain. Ableton. Said values — exact.

## Layers (each covering a band: "low, low-mid, mid-high, two highs")
1. **Kick under the snare** (same kick as the beat), EQ'd to **low + low-mid only** = low-frequency "leader".
2. Clap 1: OTT (time **667%**, downward **24%**), 36:1 compressor squash (flattened, not decaying), level way down, **HPF 550 Hz**, -2 dB at 4.17 kHz; **faded in** ("don't stack transients; low layer leads, one snare transient leads mids/highs").
3. Clap 2: faded in, unprocessed.
4. Snare with **high-pitched fundamental** ("plastic bucket with a mallet"), lows tapered.
5. **Same snare pitched -12 st, transient faded off** = low-mid thump.
- Overdrive 50% wet, dynamics 50%, band freq **1.36 kHz**.
## Group rack (parallel chains)
- Dry: Pro-Q4 **dynamic boosts at ~200 Hz (+13.65 dB range) and 2-4 kHz** (input-driven: accent fundamental and "smack"). Avoid >10 kHz boosts.
- Reverb chain 100% wet: **input low-cut 1.54 kHz**, decay **~800 ms**, diffusion damping below 400 Hz, extra HPF 403 Hz.
- **Pan chain: Corpus (Membrane/"Mima"? — chooses non-Beam), tuned to D2 (kick D0, same pitch class), decay 325 ms** -> EQ band-limit **318 Hz – 4.81 kHz** ("without it, spoons rattling") -> peak compressor (1 ms, 4:1) -> reverb 32% wet, ~1 s.
- Glue compressor 4:1 (under 10 dB GR) + makeup; soft-clip saturation ~30%. Reverse-snare swell (middle slice reversed, faded) leading into the hit.
## Takeaways
- Tearout frying-pan = resonator (membrane mode, ~300 ms decay) tuned to the track/kick pitch class, band-passed 300 Hz-5 kHz, parallel to dry stack.
- Kick low-mids as the snare's body in tearout.
