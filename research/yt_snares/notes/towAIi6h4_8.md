# towAIi6h4_8 — Kasanov, "How to make a riddim snare"
Archetype: riddim snare from kick-click + claps. Sample layering. Said.
- Layers: **kick transients** (4 kicks, sub/tail cut off, short fade-out, "crunchy... clipping — that crunchiness is what a riddim snare is"), **claps** with reverb tails cut, fades.
- Riddim claps have a **vowel formant ("ah")**: find it with a narrow EQ boost.
- Saturator on kick-transient layer for crunch; volume fade so the start is only kick, then fades into claps.
## Takeaways
- Riddim snare = kick click (low thump) + dry claps + clipping crunch, formant peak in the clap. Mostly dry (reverb optional).
