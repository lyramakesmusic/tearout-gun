# mP8O1Zi7TSs — Dripment, "How to make PERFECT SNARES for Dubstep (Snare Theory 101)"
Archetype: tearout/riddim sample selection/layering. Said, no numbers.
- Three roles: **transient "beef" layer** (loud, super-fast attack), clap layer, **tonal layer — in tearout specifically pan snares**; glue compressor.
- **Don't stack reverbs**: if each layer has baked reverb, they pile up and sound bad. Control the space; reverb once.
- 1-5 layers all fine.
- **Tune the snare**: find the loudest peak with EQ analyzer, read its note (e.g. F2) and check it's in the song scale.
- Riddim: kick is layered *under* the snare on beat 3, so the snare is already sidechained by the kick.
