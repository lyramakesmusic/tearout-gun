# Q9EuEEmIgoI — EEK!, "How To Make a Dubstep Snare In Sytrus"
Archetype: dubstep synth snare via resample loop (Seamless-R method). FL Sytrus. Said.
- Sine op, ratio 0 -> **fixed Hz**; **sine phase inverted "for more snap"**. Pitch env amount high (Sytrus range ~4 oct), starts high and drops; fundamental "**~200 Hz, up to 500 depending on snare type**"; tension controls snappiness; step-mode pitch env for odd textures.
- **Self-FM (operator modulating itself)** for overtones.
- Light distortion -> Maximus (compression, low band expanded).
- **Resample, trim leading silence ("every millisecond counts")**, reload in sampler; **sharpening the sample's attack/peak -> more ringing "realistic" snare**.
- Add white noise (volume env), Vengeance clap (mid snap), tune; OTT (mids exaggerated), distortion; repeat resample -> layer -> compress -> resample several times. Fill all frequency regions (tone, mids from claps, highs from noise/hats/foley).
