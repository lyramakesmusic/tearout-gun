# 0Z6IBXF1Yn0 — Convexity, "How to make beep snares like Convexity"
Archetype: color-bass/meme "beep" snare (tonal sine snare). FL + Serum. Said values.
- **Transient layer**: tiny click chopped from a sample, pitched way up.
- **Body: two sine oscillators an octave apart**, triggered as **two MIDI notes offset in time (flam)** — "creates this flam effect".
- Group: transient shaper (attack up, speed fast), optional Slap (snap/tight), optional **disperser chain (10x) for "laser"** transient.
- **OTT: output +13 dB, time 1000%, amount 70%.**
- **Hard clipper**, then **linear-phase EQ after the clipper** cutting below the fundamental (controls low artifacts), utility gain-down, second clipper.
## Takeaways: color bass beep snare = pure tonal sines (octave pair) + flam + click; disperser for laser zap.
