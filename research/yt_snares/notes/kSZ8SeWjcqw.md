# kSZ8SeWjcqw — Producertech (BAR9 course excerpt), "Making Fat Snares in Dubstep"
Archetype: BAR9-style dubstep snare mix processing. Said.
- Snare body hits **~200 Hz (200-300, 250 ideal)**; small boost there only (too much = peaky).
- Cut extreme highs after repitching (spurious HF clicks stop the limiter pushing harder); **HPF ~100 Hz**.
- Reverse-snare pre-swell layer.
- **Clap layer through 100%-wet reverb (R-Verb)** as the tail: not after the transient, "noisy qualities that translate well when the reverb tail is compressed".
