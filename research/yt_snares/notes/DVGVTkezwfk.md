# DVGVTkezwfk — KULTURE Samples, "How To Make UNIQUE DnB Snares"
Archetype: DnB synth snare, single Serum patch with macros (tone / noise length / punch length) + clip chain. Said values.
- LFO1 (env mode, unsynced, fast attack) -> **osc A level AND coarse pitch** (same shape: as volume drops pitch drops). LFO2 -> noise level. Macros on LFO rates = punch length / noise length.
- **Env3 very short -> global master tune, +36 st** = click ("more clicky"); osc tuned -1 st.
- FX: **Distortion Tube** (or sine-shaper/asym/hard/soft clip; "tweak after processing"), **multiband compressor** threshold down, **EQ: HPF no higher than 200 Hz; big wide boost ~1 kHz — "low mids is where the snare comes to life"**.
- Filter: HP with slight resonance (boosts body tone) + drive. Widening: Hyper/Dimension, reverb (small size, decay 1-2 s, low+high cut, "dirty" with spin depth), chorus 1-16 kHz.
- **Filter mode "Reverb" (Serum comb-like filter) with macro on cutoff = "tone"**: "different snare, different snare" — big character changer.
- One-shot noise, keytracked noise + osc. Noise: pink, "air cans", "AC hum".
- Post: **soft clipper + inflator** ceiling; KHS transient shaper (attack up, sustain down = tight); **LA-2A-style comp: threshold ~-10 dB, ratio 4, attack 15 ms** (lets a ~15 ms snap through); steep linear-phase HPF.
- Character: freq shifter / pitch shifter then re-distort (MiSstortion soft/hard clip) with EQ boost before distortion to bring back tonality ("doom doom"); static flanger as comb filter; short small-room convolution reverb low mix.
