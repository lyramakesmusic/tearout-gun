# ieENsb2vAKo — Slime Cinema, "Metallic Dariacore Snare Tutorial" (Never Ending Drumkit 23)
Archetype: metallic hyperpop/dariacore snare (SOPHIE-adjacent). FL. Said.
- Four layers: **trap snare body** (pitched up, high boost + body boost, clappy mids cut), **clap** (boosted, clipped, tail cut tight; fills the mids cut from layer 1 — complementary EQ), **metal 1** (foley -> **ring modulation** -> slight timestretch -> clip -> stereo delay, shortened; bitcrusher, OTT; enters right after the clap), **metal 2** (coffee-can/pan hit -> ring mod with frequency **set to complement the snare pitch**, pitched down).
- Metal-2 chain: parallel split: chorus || pitch-shifter+phase distortion; sum -> small room reverb -> **gate** (tight).
- Bus: **convolver using another drum as IR** (adds width/reverb), OTT (high time, low depth), clipper.
## Takeaways
- Metal layers arrive just after clap/transient; ring-mod frequency tuned to snare pitch; gated after reverb.
