# Fks15MXxwas — In The Mix, "Make Your Own Snare Drums – Sound Design Tutorial" (Serum/Sytrus)
Archetype: generic electronic snare (baseline/beginner). Said values.
- Sine a few octaves below middle C; **amp attack ~1 ms ("no click"), sustain -inf, decay ~100 ms**, curve tension for transient vs body.
- Pitch env2 -> coarse pitch unipolar (from higher down to base).
- Noise ("Alpha noise") blended. Filter **HP 18 dB** with drive (avoid kick-ness).
- **Reverb mix enveloped: starts full and shuts off within ~500 ms** ("adds harmonic energy, depth") — a gated-reverb trick inside the synth. Reverb small size, low cut.
- Distortion (filter-drive mode) mix down / drive up. Compressor **attack ~100 ms lets the transient smash through** (fast attack = laid-back snare).
- EQ **+few dB at 2-4 kHz**.
