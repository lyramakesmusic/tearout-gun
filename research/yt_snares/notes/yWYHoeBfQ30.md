# yWYHoeBfQ30 — bayesonic (Swlex), "More than Color Bass?! Using Hollow for snare design"
Archetype: color-bass "pan"/ringing snare. Said.
- Four parts: **body, transient, tail, ringing**.
- Body: Serum **triangle + noise**, env on level, env on pitch, distortion, EQ; glue comp; resampled, fade-in/out.
- Transient: sample. Tail: frequency-shifted.
- **Ringing**: a snare sample, fundamental EQ'd out (tail only) -> **Hollow resonator** tuned: **body fundamental F, ringing tuned F# (one semitone above), octave 3**; resonator spectrum loaded from a glass-hit sample (harmonic distribution). EQ boost on the ring.
- Glue comp, transient boost, clipper.
## Takeaway: ring resonator tuned **+1 semitone** above the body pitch class (same as SPORTMODE's "1, 4 or 7 st").
