# 2JviQsn94kU — neutrinia (Luna), "How to make a tearout pan snare"
Archetype: tearout pan snare. FL. Said only, no numbers.

Method 1 (3 layers): **clap** (OTT -> distortion -> reverb -> waveshaper with a point pulled to bottom so it "scrunches at the end"), **tonal layer** (bitcrush -> **ring mod** -> transient shaper -> clean reverb (Fruity Reverb, "less tone"), more distortion after reverb, low cut), **kick** (resampled, **pitched up**, bass boosted). Bus: soundgoodizer/transient shaper.
Method 2: snare -> parallel path through **phaser with sweep/LFO at 0 (static), 100% wet** -> two distortions; mix with dry -> reverb -> waveshaper -> low cut before **frequency shifter** -> transient shaper -> narrower reverb width -> EQ.
## Takeaways
- Pan tone sources: ring-mod of a tonal hit; **static phaser (fixed notches = comb-ish resonances) + distortion** on a snare. Low cut before freq shifter.
- A pitched-up kick under the snare supplies the "body/impact" in tearout.
