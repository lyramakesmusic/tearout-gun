# VWrXKHYOpTo — SPORTMODE, "the trap snare tutorial" (resonant-peak / ISOxo snare)
Archetype: hybrid trap / xdm "resonant peak" snare. Serum + samples. Said values — very decision-relevant.

## Three layers: **fundamental** (girth/tone), **clap** (energy), **resonant peak** (flare/character).
- **Fundamental layer (Serum)**: noise with "really sharp transient that gets out of the way and then the noise comes back a little" (spike-dip-swell shape, filtered); **sine for tone**; **second osc with FM, up 4 octaves** ("annoying alone, nice together"). Good fundamental samples have a clean sine at start in **200-800 Hz**; pitch a too-low dubstep snare up (~+7) to make it DnB-snappy. Tuned to D#.
- **Clap layers**: long-tail "stadium" claps (samples sound more organic than synth), plus **breath samples** and **glass-break textures**; **fades + small offsets so the fundamental has all the space**; mono'd then re-widened with chorus. Group: multiband comp, glue, soft sine/sat, HF air, low cut.
- **Resonant peak layer**: any sharply resonant tonal hit (glass, spoon, bass loop) -> low cut -> **Corpus** (resonator: "brings tails out, super resonant") -> **soothe in Delta mode** (keeps only the resonances) -> reverb -> saturator -> EQ boost peak.
  - **Tune the peak 1, 4, or 7 semitones above the fundamental pitch class** (e.g. fund D#, peak E). **Keep peaks at 1-3 kHz.**
  - **Start it slightly later** than the transient.
  - **Inverse EQ cut on the clap group at the peak frequency** so they don't compete.
- Group: transient shaper boost. Reverb tail shaped after freezing.
## Takeaways
- Modern trap/xdm snare = tuned body (200-800 Hz) + clap + resonated narrow peak 1-3 kHz at an interval (m2/M3/P5) above the body, slightly delayed.
