# MjWB8rZD3nQ — Mutrix, "How to Make a Dubstep Snare"
Archetype: Skrillex-era sample-layered dubstep snare. Said.
- Punch layer: snare with energy **180-250 Hz** ("punch you in the chest"), peak ~200 Hz, high-cut.
- High layer: 808-ish snare as-is; third: duplicate with lows cut, more highs, compressor.
- **Dubstep/DnB snares are quick; short fades, no long tail** on layers; tail from reverb send.
- Group: transient designer (Oxford TransMod ratio/overshoot), compression, EQ boost **~160 Hz**.
- Resample, second pass of transient shaping + BBE sonic maximizer.
- Skrillex snare: reverb on return 100% wet, **HPF on reverb**, compressor to bring it up; "almost sounds like a cymbal hitting".
