# tNF9zD3SzzU — Cure Audio, "2012 Called... They Want Their Snares Back" (classic brostep Skrillex snare)
Archetype: 2010-12 brostep "doofy" snare. Ableton, samples. Said.
- Waveform reading of ripped Skrillex snares (Scary Monsters etc.): **noisy transient section first, then a sine-shaped "doofy" body that comes in after the transient**, then white noise. Scary Monsters' body barely fades (sustained, compressed); the other fades straight into noise. Snares look **flat/compressed ("fat")**.
- Skrillex said he built snares from **909s**. Recipe: 909 snare -> OTT (longer time = more snap; too fast = distortion/wavefolding) -> saturator until warm (gentle HPF first, low end makes saturation buzzy; don't cut too much or lose punch) -> cut an annoying mid region, air boost -> **small reverb, short decay, pre-delay up** -> narrow **peak boost** on body.
- **Transient**: any short sound (clap, pitched-up snare, white noise), low-cut, **limited flat**, lined up at start; **fade in the body after the transient**.
- Mid harmonics: acoustic snare (Ludwig) faded in so upper-mid "odd harmonics" cut through. **Check polarity/phase between tuned layers** (cancellation loses weight).
- **Tail: China cymbal sample**, gentle low cut, shifted, fade; group -> compressor / OTT -> optional baked reverb -> **soft clip**.
## Takeaways: brostep = transient (noise) -> delayed sine body (~200 Hz, compressed/clipped, sustained) -> cymbal/noise tail. China cymbal as tail metal.
