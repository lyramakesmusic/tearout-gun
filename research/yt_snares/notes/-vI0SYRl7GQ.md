# -vI0SYRl7GQ — Code: Pandorum, "Create the PERFECT DRUMS for Dubstep, Deathstep, Riddim"
Archetype: tearout/deathstep 3-4 part layered snare (impact / tail / pan). FL. Said only. Companion to N1DFAI3LXoY (same creator; count once).

- Snare = **3 or 4 parts**: impact (click), tail, pan, optional.
- **Impact**: "something reminiscent of the click of a kick" — take the snare's click only, normalize, shorten; EQ **boost the lowest spectral peak (fundamental)**; **mono**; transient processor (attack +100%, release -100%, "sharp"); soft clipper.
- **Tail**: low cut everything that could interfere with the impact; **boost 1.6k-2 kHz** ("the sweet spot for nice snares... the sound you hear in the mix"); reverb (stereo image); optional distortion; shorten so it fades out earlier.
- **Pan snare**: any snare -> low cut -> find a spectral peak, **make a very narrow EQ bell and boost hard**; **snap the bell frequency to the song key** (FL EQ "key" snap, e.g. D) -> pan tone in key; then **distort**, lower level, EQ again to remove low-end fragments after distortion.
- Kick processing for reference: mono, transient processor 100%/-100%, soft clipper at 0 dB threshold.
## Takeaways
- Pan resonance should be key-tracked (tuned to the song key).
- Impact mono, tail stereo via reverb, everything but impact high-passed.
