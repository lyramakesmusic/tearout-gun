# OCdjEgBedtg — Ceptre, "How To Make Expressive Electronic Snares"
Archetype: DnB/bass snares (small & heavy), Kick 3 / Serum + acoustic layer. Said.

- Fundamental: **sine + pitch envelope + volume envelope**. Pitch env peaks very high (~20 kHz) with **very quick** transient decay; length short.
- **"Count wave cycles"**: "even one wave cycle too long or too short changes the vibe"; heavy snares: **~5-6 cycles** of body oscillation. Lock oscillator phase.
- **Acoustic snare layer pitched UP** (DnB snare timbre comes from sped-up breaks), **faded in** so the synthetic transient "pops pristine without competing"; low cut makes it "more synthetic/musical"; room reflections detuned 5-7 st.
- **Saturation (Saturn) to square-off dynamics** — slight flat-topping; intermodulation thump adds life.
- **Noise layer: delayed attack** "to not overlap with the transient or the fundamental — one of the biggest things in sound design": separate frequencies in time (micro-sequence: click -> body -> noise). Noise env very quick (~1/16 or shorter). Velocity -> noise. HPF on noise. Saturator after can flatten transient (sometimes better: less "blobby").
- Heavy version: **fundamental tuned to track root (D#/E)**, pitch variation per hit (higher = tonal, lower = physical); separate pitched "plop" harmonic for transient; real-life recording (fizz) as noise.
- Reference by waveform: measure transient length, body length, when noise comes up.
