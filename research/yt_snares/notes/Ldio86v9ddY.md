# Ldio86v9ddY — Mr. Bill, "Ableton Tutorial 60: How To Make Snares"
Archetype: neuro-type synthesized snare in Operator rack. Said values.
- Two Operators: **"donk"** (sine, **fixed frequency ~200-300 Hz, chose ~235-300 Hz**; fixed so the snare pitch is the same for every note — "changing the snare fundamental makes it much harder to mix") and **noise** ("wires", **slightly longer and quieter than donk**).
- Noise split in rack: **dry noise + "clap" chain (noise -> reverb -> glue -> EQ brighter)**, clap barely audible ("adds character"), reverb should have less pre-delay.
- Donk -> saturator (drive). Sum -> **saturator + glue compressor** = "turning into one of those neuro snares".
- Multiband: compress mids ~15 dB then make it back up; slow attack lets mids/highs attack through; dry/wet blend.
- **Vocoder with internal noise carrier** (formant-shaped noise follower of the snare) layered lightly for extra noise.
- **Corpus resonator: Membrane, tuned to donk freq (~235 Hz) or a multiple, adjust brightness/inharmonic, dry/wet small** -> "metallic type snares".
- Macro the donk frequency and Corpus tune together; macro decays.
- Layer foley (sticks, brushes) a bit before/after.
## Takeaways: membrane resonator tuned to body = metallic neuro ring; snare pitch is fixed (not keytracked) in his approach.
